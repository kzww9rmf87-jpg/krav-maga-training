# DUMBBELL BENCH PRESS

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Dumbbell Bench Press is a bilateral horizontal pressing exercise performed with independent implements from a supine position.

Within CAS, it is the primary loaded horizontal press available to an athlete without a barbell or rack.

---

# Philosophy

Independent implements remove the fixed bar path.

Each side must produce and stabilize its own force, which exposes asymmetry and increases the stabilizing demand at the shoulder. The load is also released safely without a spotter, which matters to an athlete training alone.

CAS selects it when upper-body muscular development is requested and dumbbells are available.

# Coaching Cues

Set the shoulder blades down and back before the first repetition.

Keep the wrists stacked over the elbows.

Lower under control to the level of the chest.

Press both implements at the same rate.

Keep the ribs down.

---

# Common Errors

One side pressing ahead of the other.

Elbows flaring toward ninety degrees.

Bouncing out of the bottom position.

Losing scapular position under fatigue.

Uncontrolled set-up or release of the implements.

---

# Performance Indicators

Repetitions completed at a controlled tempo.

Symmetry between sides maintained.

Range of motion sustained under fatigue.

Load progressed over successive sessions.

---

# Scientific Evidence

Evidence Level

★★★★

Horizontal pressing is extensively researched for upper-body muscular development, and dumbbell variations are documented as producing comparable muscular development to barbell variations at lower absolute loads.

Evidence for a distinct stabilizing benefit from independent implements is more limited and is recorded as such.

Transfer to striking performance is indirect and stated as expert consensus.

---

# Explainability

Example

The Dumbbell Bench Press is selected because the athlete requested upper-body muscular development and has dumbbells but no barbell.

Independent implements load the horizontal press at a level a bodyweight movement cannot reach, and the load can be released safely by an athlete training alone.

# Final Principle

Two implements, moving independently,

make each side accountable for its own force.

The Dumbbell Bench Press loads the horizontal press honestly,

and lets an athlete training alone put it down safely.
