# EXERCISE CLASSIFICATION

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Exercise Classification System organizes every exercise within the Combat Athlete System.

Its purpose is not simply to categorize exercises.

Its purpose is to allow intelligent reasoning.

Exercises are grouped according to their functional role,

their biomechanical characteristics,

their physiological adaptations,

and their transfer to combat performance.

---

# Philosophy

No single classification is sufficient.

Every exercise belongs simultaneously to multiple categories.

The CAS classification system is therefore multidimensional.

Each dimension contributes to decision making.
