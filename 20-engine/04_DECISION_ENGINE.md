# DECISION ENGINE

Version 0.1

---

# Purpose

The Decision Engine is the intelligence of the Combat Athlete System.

Its purpose is to transform knowledge into decisions.

It does not store scientific information.

It does not define physiological principles.

It evaluates the current situation of the athlete and determines the most appropriate training response.

The Decision Engine is responsible for answering one question:

> "What should this athlete do today?"

---

# Core Philosophy

Every recommendation is contextual.

The best session for one athlete may be the wrong session for another.

Likewise, the best session for the same athlete may change from one day to the next.

The Decision Engine therefore never follows a rigid program.

It continuously adapts decisions according to available information.

---

# Inputs

The Decision Engine evaluates four categories of information.

## Athlete Profile

Permanent information.

Examples

- Age
- Height
- Weight
- Training experience
- Primary objective
- Sport
- Injury history

---

## Current State

Temporary information.

Examples

- Sleep
- Fatigue
- Motivation
- Muscle soreness
- Pain
- Stress
- Recovery

---

## Training History

Recent workload.

Examples

- Previous sessions
- Weekly volume
- Neural load
- Recovery trends
- Missed sessions

---

## Environment

External constraints.

Examples

- Available equipment
- Session duration
- Location
- Weather
- Competition
- Travel

# Decision Hierarchy

Every decision follows the same priority.

1.

Safety

↓

2.

Recovery

↓

3.

Movement Quality

↓

4.

Primary Adaptation

↓

5.

Secondary Adaptations

↓

6.

Performance

The hierarchy is absolute.

Performance never overrides safety.

---

# Decision Process

The engine follows five sequential steps.

## Step 1

Collect information.

---

## Step 2

Detect constraints.

Examples

Pain

Fatigue

Limited time

Equipment unavailable

---

## Step 3

Determine today's priority.

Examples

Strength

Power

Recovery

Movement

Conditioning

---

## Step 4

Select Capability Modules.

Modules are selected before exercises.

---

## Step 5

Generate the final session.

Exercises are chosen only after modules have been validated.

---

# Adaptation Rules

The Decision Engine always follows these principles.

Adaptation before fatigue.

Quality before quantity.

Consistency before intensity.

Recovery before overload.

Long-term progression before short-term performance.

---

# Recovery Logic

Recovery is an active decision.

Possible outputs include

Proceed normally.

Reduce volume.

Reduce intensity.

Replace module.

Mobility only.

Zone 2 session.

Complete rest.

Doing less is sometimes the correct decision.

---

# Exercise Selection

The Decision Engine never prefers an exercise because of habit.

Exercises are selected according to

Desired adaptation

Transfer score

Recovery cost

Technical complexity

Equipment availability

Athlete proficiency

---

# Learning

The engine continuously learns from athlete feedback.

Examples

Session difficulty.

Recovery time.

Performance trends.

Pain reports.

Subjective readiness.

Future recommendations improve as data accumulates.

---

# Explainability

Every recommendation must be explainable.

The athlete should always understand

Why this session?

Why these exercises?

Why this volume?

Why today?

The system never behaves like a black box.

---

# Constraints

The engine must never recommend a session that violates fundamental CAS principles.

Examples

High neural fatigue + maximal power.

Pain ignored.

Recovery ignored.

Unnecessary complexity.

If uncertainty exists, the safest recommendation is preferred.

---

# Success Criteria

The Decision Engine succeeds when

the athlete consistently makes better training decisions than they would have made alone.

The quality of the engine is not measured by complexity.

It is measured by the relevance of its decisions.

---

# Final Principle

Knowledge without decisions has little value.

The purpose of the Combat Athlete System is not only to understand training.

Its purpose is to decide intelligently.

The Decision Engine is therefore the heart of the application.

Everything else exists to support it.
