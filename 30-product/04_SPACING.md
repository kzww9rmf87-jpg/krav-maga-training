# SPACING

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

Spacing creates clarity.

Not decoration.

The athlete should immediately understand:

What belongs together.

What is important.

What can be ignored.

Good spacing reduces cognitive load before the athlete even reads the interface.

White space is never wasted space.

---

# Base Unit

CAS uses a 4-point spacing system.

Every spacing value is a multiple of 4.

This creates visual rhythm and consistency.

---

# Spacing Scale

XXS

4 px

Very small gaps.

Icons and labels.

---

XS

8 px

Related elements.

Small vertical spacing.

---

S

12 px

Compact groups.

Form elements.

---

M

16 px

Default spacing.

Most layouts.

Cards.

Lists.

Buttons.

---

L

24 px

Section spacing.

Large groups.

---

XL

32 px

Major separation.

Between independent sections.

---

XXL

48 px

Screen-level spacing.

Major visual breaks.

---

# Screen Margins

Horizontal margin

24 px

All primary screens.

Never less than 20 px.

Never more than 32 px.

---

Vertical margin

24 px

Top and bottom breathing room.

---

# Cards

Internal padding

20 px

Never less than 16 px.

---

Spacing between cards

16 px

Cards should never touch.

---

Grouped cards

8 px

Only when cards represent the same object.

---

# Buttons

Internal horizontal padding

24 px

---

Internal vertical padding

16 px

---

Spacing between buttons

12 px

---

Primary button spacing

24 px

Enough room to avoid accidental taps.

---

# Lists

Spacing between list items

12 px

---

Grouped list spacing

8 px

---

Spacing between list sections

24 px

---

# Forms

Spacing between input fields

16 px

---

Spacing between labels and inputs

8 px

---

Spacing between form sections

32 px

---

# Exercise Screen

Exercise name

24 px above.

16 px below.

---

Load

16 px below exercise name.

---

Repetitions

12 px below load.

---

Timer

24 px below repetitions.

---

Controls

32 px below timer.

Training screens should breathe.

Nothing should feel crowded.

---

# Statistics

Spacing above graph

24 px

---

Spacing below graph

20 px

---

Spacing between metrics

16 px

---

Spacing between graph sections

32 px

---

# Icons

Spacing between icon and text

8 px

Never less.

Never more.

---

# Navigation

Spacing between navigation items

16 px

---

Bottom safe area

Always respected.

Never overlap system gestures.

---

# Touch Targets

Minimum touch area

44 × 44 px

Preferred

48 × 48 px

Training often happens with sweaty hands.

Large targets improve reliability.

---

# Density

CAS prefers comfortable layouts.

Never attempt to maximize information density.

More information does not create more understanding.

If two elements compete for attention,

increase spacing.

---

# Consistency

Similar situations require similar spacing.

Never invent custom spacing for a single screen.

Every spacing value should come from the spacing scale.

No arbitrary numbers.

No visual guessing.

---

# Final Principle

Spacing should make the interface feel effortless.

If the athlete notices the spacing,

it is probably wrong.

Good spacing becomes invisible.

Only clarity remains.
